Intermediate Development | Building Better Study Plans

Mock Session & Peer Review

Put your study planning skills into practice through a recorded mock session, then strengthen your coaching perspective by reviewing a peer’s session.

01

What you've learned so far

Throughout this learning path, you've explored how to help students build personalized study plans without taking ownership of their learning away from them.

Recognize

Identify when planning is useful

Recognize opportunities for a study plan, including when a student is beginning a course, preparing for an assessment, or working toward a retake.

Collaborate

Build a plan with the student

Use coaching questions to explore goals, available time, course topics, learning resources, and possible barriers. Keep the conversation collaborative—not an advising session.

Personalize

Make the plan realistic and actionable

Break larger goals into manageable targets, identify days and time blocks, include appropriate learning supports, and establish useful check-ins and accountability.

Empower

Support student ownership

Help students make decisions about their own plan, practice using it, and leave with clear next steps they feel confident carrying out.

02

Your mock session scenario

A student starting a new course

You'll coach a student who is brand new to the course and has no prior knowledge of its topics. Your goal is to collaboratively create a realistic study plan that helps them get started, identify resources, and determine what to work on and when.

No KWL chart for this scenario. Because the student is unfamiliar with the course topics, a KWL study strategy is not needed. Instead, focus on exploring the course structure, identifying resources, and establishing a manageable starting plan.

03

How the peer review process works

You will complete a mock session that will be recorded. Instead of receiving live feedback from the development program supervisor or a senior peer navigator, you will exchange feedback with another peer navigator who is completing this same learning path.

Step 1

Complete your recorded mock

Choose the appointment or live event environment that fits your coaching role. Facilitate a mock session focused on collaboratively building a study plan. Your session will be recorded for peer review.

Step 2

Receive a peer recording

After your mock, you will be sent a recording of another peer navigator's mock session from this learning path. Assignment timing depends on when peers complete their own sessions.

Step 3

Submit anonymous feedback

Once you receive your assigned recording, watch it and continue to the next page in Canvas to submit your review. Your feedback will be shared anonymously with the peer you reviewed.

When will I receive a peer recording?

It may take anywhere from one day to two weeks after your mock session, depending on the progress of other peer navigators in this learning path. You do not need to submit peer feedback until you receive your assigned recording.

Review with a growth mindset. Identify effective coaching practices and provide specific, constructive suggestions grounded in the study planning skills covered in this learning path. Do not share mock session recordings with others without the permission of the peer navigator in the mock session.

04

Special guidance for live event coaches

This mock is intentionally streamlined so you can concentrate on helping students create and practice a study plan.

AFFIRM · Step 1 of this mock

Frame the Session

Set the expectation that the group will work collaboratively to develop realistic study plans for a course they are just beginning.

AFFIRM · Step 2 of this mock

Focus on the Strategy

Introduce the purpose and key components of a study plan, modeling how to use the course overview, resources, time blocks, and goals to plan effectively.

AFFIRM · Step 3 of this mock

Intentional Practice

Facilitate hands-on planning with both students. Invite them to make choices, explain their thinking, and leave with actionable steps they have selected themselves.

Only these three AFFIRM steps are included. Skip the other three AFFIRM steps for this mock. No discussion map is required. You are welcome to create a discussion map if you feel it is necessary. These adjustments apply to the practice mock, not the full live event format.
