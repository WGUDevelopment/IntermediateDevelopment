Peer Intern Development Program

Building Better Study Plans

Strengthen your ability to facilitate collaborative, individualized study planning conversations that empower students to take ownership of their learning, make informed decisions, and establish meaningful accountability.

Intermediate Level Estimated Duration: 5.5 Hours Completion Period: 6 Weeks

Learning Objectives

By the end of this learning path, you will be able to:

  • ✓ Recognize opportunities for building study plans with students.
  • ✓ Apply the KWL strategy when appropriate to help students make informed decisions about their study priorities.
  • ✓ Facilitate collaborative study planning conversations that empower students to make decisions.
  • ✓ Integrate ongoing coaching and accountability by helping students identify opportunities to monitor progress and engage in follow-up sessions.

What You'll Do

Throughout this learning path, you will explore effective study planning practices, apply what you have learned, and strengthen your skills through practice and peer feedback.

1
Reading

Recognizing the Need

Explore opportunities to incorporate study planning into coaching sessions and recognize when a student may benefit from developing or revisiting a study plan.

2
Video Walkthrough

Starting with What Students Know

Explore how the KWL chart can be used to help students identify what they already know, recognize gaps in their understanding, and make informed decisions about their study priorities.

3
Reading & Assignment

Building the Plan Together

Explore the components of an effective study plan and learn how to use the study plan template to facilitate a collaborative planning conversation. Apply what you have learned by completing and submitting a study plan.

4
Mock Session & Peer Review

Putting It Into Practice

Demonstrate your ability to facilitate a collaborative study planning conversation by completing a mock coaching session and receiving peer feedback. You will also review a peer's mock session and provide constructive feedback on their application of the skills explored throughout this learning path.

Additional Things to Know

Required Assignment

If you have been added to this learning path, your leadership has assigned it as a required development activity.

Complete in Order

Complete each step before moving on to the next. Finish the reading and video walkthrough before completing the study plan assignment, and complete the assignment before moving on to the mock session and peer review activities.

Prioritize Direct-Leader Tasks

Tasks assigned by your direct leader take priority over Development Program learning path activities. Plan your learning path work around those responsibilities.
