Starting with What Students Know

Using the KWL Chart

Before developing a study plan, it can be helpful to explore what the student already knows and what they still need to learn. The KWL chart is one tool you can use to facilitate this conversation, helping you and the student collaboratively identify learning priorities and create a more intentional, individualized study plan.
