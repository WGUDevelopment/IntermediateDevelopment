Navigating Content Conversations

Strengthen your ability to navigate conversations about course content while maintaining the integrity of the coaching relationship, promoting independent learning, and empowering students to develop their own understanding.

Welcome to Your Learning Path

As a Peer Navigator, you will frequently encounter situations in which students express confusion, ask questions about course content, or seek confirmation of their understanding. Navigating these conversations effectively requires intentional language, thoughtful redirection, and a clear understanding of the distinction between coaching and teaching.

Throughout this learning path, you will explore the importance of maintaining non-teaching boundaries, evaluate realistic coaching scenarios, and practice strategies that help students strengthen their comprehension without taking ownership of their learning.

Estimated Duration
6.75 hours over a 7-week period

Learning Objectives

Upon completion of this learning path, you will be able to:

  1. Evaluate coaching conversations to identify behaviors that cross into content instruction.
  2. Demonstrate an understanding of how non-teaching boundaries protect the integrity of the coaching relationship.
  3. Apply intentional redirection techniques to navigate student questions about course content.
  4. Apply the Feynman Technique to facilitate student understanding without transitioning into content instruction.

What You'll Do

This learning path consists of six steps designed to build your understanding of non-teaching boundaries and provide opportunities to apply what you have learned in realistic coaching situations.

1

Consequences of Teaching During Coaching

Reading

Explore the consequences of crossing into content instruction during coaching and understand why maintaining the distinction between coaching and teaching is essential for students, Peer Navigators, and our professional partnerships.

2

Staying in the Coaching Lane

Asynchronous Presentation

Watch a recorded presentation introducing strategies for navigating content conversations, recognizing potential teaching moments, and intentionally redirecting conversations without transitioning into content instruction.

3

The Language of Effective Coaching

Application Activity

Explore how intentional language influences the coaching experience. Practice transforming teaching-oriented statements and questions into coaching language that promotes student ownership and independent learning.

4

Find the Teaching Moment

Written Response Video Quiz

Evaluate realistic coaching scenarios, identify moments when a Peer Navigator crosses into content instruction, and explain how the situation could have been approached differently.

5

Live Event Redirection

Multiple Choice Video Quiz

Explore challenging live event scenarios and determine the most appropriate response. Observe your selected responses in action and practice making intentional decisions that maintain the coaching lane while supporting student learning.

6

Using the Feynman Technique to Aid Comprehension

Video Demonstration Coaching Fishbowls

Observe a Peer Navigator using the Feynman Technique during a coaching appointment to support student comprehension without transitioning into content instruction.

Apply your learning through a three-week series of Coaching Fishbowls. You will host one simulated live event using the Feynman Technique and attend two additional sessions, participating as a student and providing constructive feedback to your peers.
