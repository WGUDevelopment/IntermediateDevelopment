Key Takeaways

Coaching through content-related questions means keeping the focus on the student's learning process rather than explaining or validating academic content.

Strategy 01

Redirect to the Student

Explore what the student already understands, where they're stuck, and how they might move forward.

Strategy 02

Redirect to Course Material

Help students locate information, reflect on what they're reading, and identify questions for their CI.

Strategy 03

Redirect to Other Students

During live events, encourage collaborative discussion without confirming or correcting answers.

Remember: A think-aloud can briefly model how you interpret or paraphrase course material as part of demonstrating a study strategy. The purpose is to model the learning process, not teach the content. Then, return the thinking to the student.

Coaching lives in curiosity and questions.

Help students develop the skills, strategies, and confidence to find answers independently.
