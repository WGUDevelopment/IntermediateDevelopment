Live Event Redirection

Apply your coaching skills in realistic live event scenarios.

Video quiz instructions

In this activity, you will watch four live event scenarios that could elicit a teaching response if the Peer Navigator is not intentional about their response.

  1. Watch each scenario and consider how you would respond while staying in the coaching lane.
  2. Choose the answer you believe is most appropriate for the situation.
  3. Watch your selected response play out in the live event.
  4. If your response is incorrect, you will return to the question and answer choices to try again. A correct response will advance you to the next scenario.
  5. After completing all four scenarios, download your results and submit the document on the next Canvas page.

Note: Only your first response to each scenario will be recorded in your results.
