Step 5: Mock Session

Learn how to invite student participation, facilitate meaningful discussion, and choose engagement techniques that help students take an active role in learning.

Step 5: Request Your Mock Session

After requesting your mock session, the Development Program Supervisor or a Senior Peer Navigator will schedule it with you.

The mock session focuses on facilitating open group discussion during the Achievement Orientation, Framing the Session, and Focusing on the Strategy sections of AFFIRM.

What Your Mock Session Observer Will Be Looking For

  • Give clear verbal and written directions.
  • Ask questions that guide critical thinking.
  • Ask follow-up questions when answers are vague.
  • Explain the reasoning behind your practice strategy.
  • Encourage student reflection, evaluation, and application.

This mock session will be in live event format. If you have never hosted a live event before, it is recommended that you find a live event to shadow before requesting your mock session. A discussion map is not necessary to prepare for this mock session, but you may use one if you choose. The course and topic of the live event is your choice. It is recommended that you use a slide presentation as visuals contribute to student engagement.
