Step 4: Open Discussion

Learn how to invite student participation, facilitate meaningful discussion, and choose engagement techniques that help students take an active role in learning.

Step 4: Facilitating Open Group Discussion

Weak Facilitation

  • Facilitator does most of the speaking.
  • Questions are surface-level or yes/no.
  • Students do not feel comfortable participating.
  • Conversation focuses on the negative.

Strong Facilitation

  • Facilitator speaks minimally.
  • Uses guiding questions.
  • Encourages students to share ideas.
  • Focuses on positive next steps.

Facilitation Phrase Examples

WeakestNo intentional space for student interaction.
Weak"Are we feeling good about moving on?"
Less Strong"Give me thumbs up or heart emoji if you feel good about moving on."
Strong"Using the mic or the chat, share your thoughts about what we've covered so far."
Strongest"Before we dive into what we're going to be covering today, let's open up the floor for everyone to share what they hope to get from today's session. You are welcome to share using the mic or chat, whichever you are most comfortable with."
