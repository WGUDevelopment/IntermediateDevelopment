Why Clear Directions Matter

In the first video, engagement is low because the facilitator did not express participation expectations.

Without clear directions, students may not feel confident about when or how to participate.

In the second video, the facilitator explains exactly how students can participate, reassuring them that they can contribute using the mic or chat.

Always explain what students should do, where they should respond, and when they should respond.
