PEER INTERN DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM | CREATING INTERACTIVE COACHING EXPERIENCES

Overview

Learn how to invite student participation, facilitate meaningful discussion, and choose engagement techniques that help students take an active role in learning.

Welcome to Your Learning Path

Interactive coaching means creating a welcoming structure where students can think, contribute, reflect, and apply their learning.

Estimated Duration
4.25 hours over a 5-week period

Learning Objectives

  1. Explain the importance of student interaction.
  2. Understand the use of clear written and verbal directions.
  3. Demonstrate the ability to guide powerful open discussion.
  4. Identify effective engagement techniques for coaching scenarios.

What You'll Do

1. Reflection

Think critically about what you've learned.

2. Workshop

Explore different engagement techniques.

3. Mock Session

Practice facilitating open discussion.

4. Check Understanding

Answer scenario-based questions.
