Step 2: Engagement Techniques Workshop

Review these techniques before attending the workshop so you can fully participate.

1. Group Discussion

Students participate verbally on mic or through the chat. This is the most common method.

2. Popcorn Responses

A fast-paced technique where students submit their responses at the same time in the chat.

3. Participant Reactions

Students use reactions or emojis to respond, vote, or show agreement.

4. Polls

Collect student responses using a poll, survey, or chat-based question. Keep polls short and focused.

5. Round Robin

Students take turns sharing ideas verbally or in the chat.
Each workshop consists of 2–5 coaches, real student scenarios, and opportunities to think critically and have conversations.
